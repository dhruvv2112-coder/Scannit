### Details

1. **SQLAlchemy `MappedAnnotationError` fix** — `services/api/app/models/tables.py`
   had bare `Mapped` annotations (without type parameters) on `created_at`,
   `updated_at`, and `completed_at` columns across all four ORM models
   (`ProjectRow`, `DatasetRow`, `JobRow`, `ArtifactRow`). SQLAlchemy 2.x
   Annotated Declarative requires `Mapped[datetime]` or `Mapped[datetime | None]`.
   This caused `test-api` CI to fail with collection errors on commits
   `ee3c4f8` and earlier (pushed by another session on 2026-11-04).

2. **Tracker audit columns** — Added `Evidence_URL`, `Acceptance_Status`,
   `Merge_SHA`, `Last_Verified_IST` to `docs/project_plan/features.csv`
   per the next-cycle integration contract requirements.
